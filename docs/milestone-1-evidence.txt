What was learned:
- A dispatch interval bounds enqueue rate but cannot alone bound queue cardinality; stable transport IDs complement Mongo-owned scheduling without turning queue state into durable business truth.
- csv-parse configuration does not substitute for application byte and Unicode-code-point limits. Fatal UTF-8 decoding and explicit line-ending policy are separate concerns.
- Conventional CSV can contain multiline fields, but this fixed contract explicitly forbids them; per-line strict parsing enforces that smaller language.
- Integer grams and full calendar round-trip checks avoid floating-point rounding and permissive date normalization.
- Strict TypeScript and executable parser tests now have hands-on evidence. NestJS, Redis/BullMQ, Mongo persistence and OIDC runtime learning has NOT yet occurred in this milestone.
